# Candidate invariants

These were review targets, not established guarantees. Statuses below reflect the verified register as of 2026-09-20 (audit paused per decision [D-003](decisions.md)): **none of the eight candidate guarantees currently holds platform-wide** — each has verified counterexamples in [the register](findings.md). Component check suites and platform E2E are green for what they assert; that is evidence for their covered cases, not for these platform-level guarantees.

| ID | Candidate guarantee | Evidence/status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| INV-01 | A successful local commit durably records both the edit and required sync metadata under the supported storage contract; old acknowledgements cannot clear newer edits. | Not established — violated by AUD-017 (MEMORY-journal crash corruption, reproduced), AUD-019 (old ACK clears newer metadata edit, reproduced), AUD-022 (failover replay duplication). |
| INV-02 | Cursors do not advance past unapplied work unless that work is durably retained for recovery. | Established at the pull boundary as of 2026-09-21 (AUD-025 fix: staged cursors commit only after application; partial streams and decrypt failures gate the cursor). Residual: per-record apply failures rely on the in-memory quarantine (see AUD-025 record). |
| INV-03 | Retries and supported delivery schedules preserve intended edits and eventually converge according to declared conflict semantics. | Not established — violated by AUD-026/AUD-032 (rewrap race masked by the DEK wire defect, the latter reproduced), AUD-027 (false upload acknowledgement), AUD-036 (permanently stuck uploads), AUD-046 (debounce draft loss, reproduced), AUD-049 (stale full-array write removes peer edits). |
| INV-04 | Authentication, authorization, key ownership, and ciphertext context binding agree across service/client boundaries; confidentiality claims identify their trusted endpoints. | Not established — violated by the three verified critical takeovers AUD-003/AUD-005/AUD-006, plus AUD-024 (revoked member decrypts future content), AUD-028 (Write authorizes admin RPCs), AUD-038 (peer rebroadcast into unrelated spaces), AUD-045 (cross-account record visibility), AUD-041 (inference plaintext path). |
| INV-05 | Interrupted or concurrent recovery, password changes, and key rotation preserve a coherent, usable key path; revocation enforces its precisely defined future-access guarantees. | Not established — violated by AUD-004 (refresh-family revocation failure, reproduced), AUD-009 (rotation lacks generation/CAS), AUD-011 (no session revocation), AUD-016 (recovery blob replaced before confirmation), AUD-024, AUD-029 (min-generation not enforced on network pushes), AUD-034 (invitations mislabel epoch). |
| INV-06 | Deletion, compaction, migrations, and backup restore have defined reconciliation behavior without unexplained resurrection or loss. | Not established — violated by AUD-017 (crash corruption of committed data), AUD-027 (file metadata/object divergence), AUD-039 (file objects never removed); restore/upgrade drills deferred (D-003). |
| INV-07 | Malformed, unauthorized, replayed, and resource-exhausting input cannot violate protected state or the declared availability bounds. | Not established — violated by AUD-021 (decoder count expansion, reproduced), AUD-014 (code-verification race, reproduced), AUD-035 (mailbox poison item), AUD-043/AUD-044 (JWKS refresh amplification; unbounded proxy streams). |
| INV-08 | Account switching and application lifecycle events honor the retention policy, preserve isolation, and expose unsaved or unsyncable state accurately. | Not established — violated by AUD-010 (logout race resurrects session, reproduced), AUD-012 (non-scoped key/session storage), AUD-045 (prior account's records visible), AUD-051 (draft loss / duplicate sends). |

Remediation should treat each violated invariant as an acceptance criterion: a guarantee flips to "established" only when its counterexample findings are fixed and carry regression evidence, and the deferred D-003 campaigns have exercised the cross-component schedules.
