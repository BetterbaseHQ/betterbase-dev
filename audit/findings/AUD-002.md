# AUD-002: Root `check-all` omits inference, json-joy-rs, and platform E2E

- Type: Platform gate gap
- State: Fixed 2026-09-20; verified by dry run; full execution pending
- Severity / rationale: green `check-all` did not cover all components
- Confidence and evidence basis: source-confirmed at baseline and HEAD
- Owner: remediation wave 0
- Affected repositories, revisions, and local patches: `betterbase-dev` fix `982b3ff` (justfile, AGENTS.md)
- Violated invariant / linked decision: plan Stage 4 release-gate criterion

## Impact and preconditions

A passing platform check left inference and the CRDT library unreviewed by the gate; E2E was a separate undocumented-in-`check-all` step.

## Evidence and reproduction

`justfile` `check-all` recipe at baseline revision; register entry AUD-002.

## Remediation

`check-all` now runs `just check` for `betterbase-inference` and `json-joy-rs` alongside the existing components. Platform E2E is deliberately not folded into `check-all` (it builds and boots the isolated e2e compose stack); instead a new `check-platform: check-all` + `just e2e` composes the full gate, and AGENTS.md documents both.

## Verification

`just -n check-all` and `just --list` parse cleanly; the two added recipes invoke the target repos' existing `just check` (unchanged; their baseline evidence records green runs). Full `check-all`/`check-platform` execution not re-run this wave. Verifier: remediation agent, 2026-09-20.
