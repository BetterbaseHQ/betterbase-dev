## Impact and preconditions

Describe the actor or failure, reachability, affected data/users, and blast radius. State uncertainty and what would disprove the finding.

## Evidence and reproduction

Link exact source locations at the affected revisions and sanitized evidence. Include expected versus actual behavior and a minimal reproduction, or a complete source argument when execution is unavailable. Record unexecuted steps honestly.

## Remediation

Describe the root cause, proposed correction, cross-repository dependencies, compatibility/migration implications, and regression test. Keep proposals distinct from implemented fixes.

## Verification

Record fix revision(s), original-reproduction result, regression/repository checks, skips, remaining limitations, verifier, and date. Do not mark verified until the relevant evidence exists.
